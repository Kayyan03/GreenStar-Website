Why we exist

The information
exists. It's just in
the wrong place.

Sustainability data for fashion is everywhere and nowhere. It sits in 80-page PDFs, in opaque ratings nobody trusts, in marketing copy that says everything and means nothing.

Meanwhile, the decision that matters (buy this, buy that, buy nothing) happens in the few seconds between landing on a product page and adding to cart.

We think that gap is the problem. Not awareness. Not intent. Friction.

GreenStar is our attempt to close it. A rating that meets you where you already are, on the product page. A score that shows its working. A better option, if a better option exists.

Principles

Four non-negotiables.

The internal compass. What we'd rather not ship than compromise on.

  • /P.01 Quiet, not loud We don't interrupt your purchase. No modals, no pop-ups, no guilt trips. One click, in your control, always. UX
  • /P.02 Show your working Every score is fully traceable. Click through to see the components, the sources, and the gaps. Data
  • /P.03 Reward transparency Brands that disclose clearly score higher. Opacity costs points. We hold the industry accountable for what they choose not to say. Values
  • /P.04 No dark patterns We don't hide the downsides. We don't dress up weak data as a strong score. Ever. Ethics
