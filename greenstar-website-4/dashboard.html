A hypothetical onboarding, built from real data. The first 30 minutes inside the dashboard, end to end.

From annual report
to actionable benchmark.

On day one, a Ganni sustainability analyst loads the dashboard and finds Ganni already scored 3.6/5. GreenStar has ingested the 2024 annual report, cross-referenced the SBTi database, pulled GOTS and Bluesign certification data, and checked the last five years of Clean Clothes Campaign incident records.

The analyst builds a custom peer set: COS, Arket, Patagonia, and Pangaia. Within two clicks, they can see that Ganni leads on Incidents and Suppliers but trails Patagonia on Standards and Transparency, and specifically lacks ISO 14001 certification that most peers have at partial coverage.

A PDF export is sent to the CEO before the 10am call. By end of week, the sustainability team has three specific investment recommendations with quantified score uplifts attached. That's the entire point.
