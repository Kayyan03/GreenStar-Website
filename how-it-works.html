How it works Four stages · One click

Read. Rate.
Redirect.

Click the extension on any fashion product page. GreenStar reads what's on the page, combines it with the brand's track record, and shows you a better option if there is one.

Stage 01 · Read

The page,
decoded.

Product pages already contain most of what we need for a sustainability rating. The hard part is pulling it out reliably across dozens of different retailer layouts. That's what the extension does automatically.

A product page is more honest than it looks. Everything we need is already there, if you know where to look.
Fields extracted
  • /01 Brand Retailer's own label or a third-party brand required
  • /02 Price Used for matching alternatives within ±20% required
  • /03 Materials Composition %, recycled content, fibre type required
  • /04 Origin Country of manufacturing, where disclosed optional
  • /05 Care labels Washing instructions, a rough proxy for longevity optional
  • /06 Claims "Organic", "recycled", named certifications optional

Stage 03 · The opacity penalty

Missing data
isn't neutral.

If a retailer chooses not to disclose sustainability signals, we reduce the score and the visual confidence. Two brands with very different levels of transparency shouldn't look identical on a product page.

Stage 04 · Redirect

The suggestion
engine.

Every low score comes with a specific alternative, not a lecture. We match on style first, then colour, then price, and only suggest something if the score is meaningfully higher. If nothing better exists, we say so.

  • /R.01 Same style Oversized hoodies map to oversized hoodies, slim trousers map to slim trousers. Shape, cut and product type all need to match. strict
  • /R.02 Same colour Visual similarity within ΔE < 20. You like black, we show black. strict
  • /R.03 Price ±20% Aspirational, but not unreachable. No £32 hoodie swapped for a £300 one. tight
  • /R.04 Higher score Minimum uplift of +0.7 points. Below that, no suggestion at all. required
  • /R.05 No paid placement The recommendation has to earn its place. principle
